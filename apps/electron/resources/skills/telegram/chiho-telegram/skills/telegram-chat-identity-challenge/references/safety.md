# Safety

- The challenge is an operational screening tool, not an identity proof.
- A refusal, delay, or awkward answer is not enough to infer nationality, employer, location, or intent.
- Keep the target of criticism to Kim Jong Un as a public political leader.
- Do not request threats, violent content, slurs, or insults against protected classes.
- Do not harass contacts who refuse; pause the workflow and ask the user to decide.
- Use preview-first messaging for all non-trivial or batch use.
- Keep audit notes factual: "refused challenge" or "needs manual verification" is acceptable; accusations are not.
