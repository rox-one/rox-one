# Safety

- This is not a real identity check and not proof that the contact is human.
- Advanced bots, scripts, or LLM-driven operators may pass simple reasoning challenges.
- Failed challenges should create manual-review signals, not accusations.
- Do not request personal data or documents as part of this workflow.
- Do not use inaccessible image-only challenges unless the user explicitly chooses that tradeoff.
- Keep audit labels factual, such as `Human Check Passed`, `Human Check Failed`, or `Needs Manual Verification`.
- For payment, credential, hiring, investor, or admin-access workflows, require manual review even after a passed challenge.
